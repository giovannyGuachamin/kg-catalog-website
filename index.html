K&G Boutique Automation

A private tool used by K&G Boutique to organize product photographs and information and prepare its commercial catalog.

How it works

An operator prepares and saves product photographs. The local application uploads images to Google Drive, records names, SKUs, prices, and descriptions in Google Sheets, and prepares a data sheet for import into the Meta catalog.

The processing workflow supports checking product status and resuming pending operations. The Google account owner authorizes access through their browser.

Use of Google services

We use Google Drive to store photographs and manage access links, and Google Sheets to maintain product, batch, and catalog data records. The current configuration requests the Drive and Sheets permissions shown on the consent screen.
