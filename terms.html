Purpose and users

The application is a private tool for managing the K&G Boutique catalog. It is intended for the business owner and operators authorized by the business.

Operator responsibilities

The operator must be authorized to use the Google account, photographs, and data being processed. They must review prices, descriptions, images, and availability for accuracy before publication, protect credentials, and maintain any backups they need.

External services

Operation depends on Google Drive, Google Sheets, ChatGPT, and Meta catalog services according to the actions used. Each service has its own terms, limits, and review procedures. Preparing or importing a product does not guarantee approval or a publication timeline.

Availability

Operations may be interrupted by network problems, usage limits, revoked authorizations, or changes to external services. The operator must review processing states and resolve outstanding errors before resuming processing.

Data and ending use

Data handling is described in the privacy policy. The account owner may stop using the application and revoke its Google access. Photographs, records, and products already published must be managed separately.
